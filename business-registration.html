Questions we often get

How long does it take to register a private limited company or LLP?

Usually two to four weeks once documents are ready, depending on name approval and MCA processing.

Should I choose an LLP or a private limited company?

An LLP is simpler and cheaper to run. A private limited company suits you better if you plan to raise investment or issue shares to employees. We'll go through your situation and recommend one.

Can a foreign national be a partner in an Indian LLP?

Yes, subject to the FDI rules for the activity and with at least one resident designated partner. We handle the incorporation and the foreign investment reporting.
