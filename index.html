Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you serve?

Clients anywhere in India, and NRIs and foreign founders abroad. Almost everything is done online, through WhatsApp, email, phone and shared folders, so where you are makes no difference. If you prefer to meet in person, you are welcome at our office.

How are your fees decided?

We quote a fixed fee before we start, based on the work involved. Government fees, challans and late fees are passed on at actual cost, with receipts. No surprises on the invoice.

Can I reach you outside office hours?

Yes. Send us a WhatsApp message or email any time, including weekends, and we reply as soon as we can, usually the same day. When a deadline is close, we stay available until the filing is done.

I am behind on my filings. Can you help?

Yes, and it is more common than you think. We list everything that is pending, work out the penalties, and bring you up to date in the order that saves you the most money.

How do I share documents with you?

Whatever is easiest for you: WhatsApp, email, a shared Google Drive folder, or drop them at our office. We send a short checklist so you know exactly what is needed.

How do you make sure deadlines are not missed?

Every client's GST, TDS, income tax and ROC due dates are on our own compliance desk, which flags what is due and what is overdue every day. You get reminders well before the due date.
