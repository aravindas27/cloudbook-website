GST registration & returns

GST is a monthly habit, not a once-a-year job. We take care of it every month so your returns go in on time, your input tax credit is matched, and late fees stop being a line item.

Get a fixed-fee quote WhatsApp us

What we handle

  • New GST registration, including documents, place-of-business proof and replies to officer queries
  • Monthly or quarterly returns: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and the QRMP scheme with invoice furnishing
  • Reconciliation of your purchases with GSTR-2B before every return, so you claim the credit you are entitled to
  • GSTR-9 annual return and reconciliation with your books
  • Letter of Undertaking (LUT) for exporters and refund applications
  • Amendments, cancellation and revocation of registration
  • Replies to notices, mismatches and scrutiny, with clear explanations of what the department is asking

How it works

  • You share sales and purchase data each month: Tally or Zoho export, Excel, or simply your invoices
  • We reconcile, prepare the return and send you a short summary of tax payable before filing
  • You pay the challan (or we pay it and recover at cost), and we file and send you the acknowledgement
  • Every due date is tracked on our compliance desk, so nothing depends on memory

Who this suits

  • Traders, manufacturers and service providers registered under GST
  • Startups and small businesses that need their first registration
  • Exporters who need an LUT every year
  • Businesses that have fallen behind and need returns brought up to date

Questions we often get

What does it cost to file GST returns every month?

It depends on the number of invoices and whether you file monthly or quarterly. Most small businesses pay a fixed monthly fee, which we quote before we start. Late fees for missed returns usually cost more than our fee.

Can you file returns I have already missed?

Yes. We file pending returns in order, work out the late fee and interest, and get your GSTIN back to a clean status.

Do I need GST registration?

Registration is compulsory once your turnover crosses the threshold for your state and type of supply, and in some cases (inter-state sales, e-commerce, specified services) regardless of turnover. Tell us what you sell and where, and we will give you a straight answer.
