Company & LLP compliance

MCA penalties add up at ₹100 a day per form, and a missed filing can disqualify directors. We run your company or LLP compliance on a calendar, so filings are prepared well before the due date.

Private limited companies

  • Board meetings and the AGM: notices, minutes and resolutions
  • AOC-4 (financial statements) and MGT-7 / MGT-7A (annual return)
  • ADT-1 for auditor appointment, DIR-3 KYC for directors
  • DPT-3, MSME-1 and INC-20A where they apply
  • Change of name, registered office, directors or share capital
  • Clearing old backlogs, including filings that were missed for several years

LLPs

  • Form 11 (annual return) by 30 May and Form 8 (statement of accounts) by 30 October
  • Designated partner KYC
  • Admission and retirement of partners, with the supplementary LLP agreement and Form 3 and Form 4 filings
  • Changes to contribution, business activity or address

Foreign-owned companies

  • Annual FLA return to the RBI for companies with foreign investment
  • Coordination of transfer pricing reports and FEMA reporting with your auditors

Questions we often get

What happens if my company does not file AOC-4 and MGT-7?

The company pays an additional fee of ₹100 a day for each form, and after continuing defaults the directors can be disqualified and the company can be struck off. If you are behind, the sooner it is fixed, the lower the cost.

Is audit compulsory for my LLP?

An LLP needs an audit if its turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh in a year. Below that, the accounts are certified by the partners. Audits are carried out by practising Chartered Accountants we work with.

Can you handle a partner leaving our LLP?

Yes. We draft the retirement and admission documents, the supplementary LLP agreement, and file Form 3 and Form 4 with the MCA.
