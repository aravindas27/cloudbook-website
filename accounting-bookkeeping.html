Accounting & bookkeeping

Good books make every other job easier: tax returns, bank loans, investor questions and your own decisions. We keep your accounts up to date every month, not in a rush at year-end.

Monthly accounting

  • Bookkeeping in Tally Prime, Zoho Books or Xero, from your bank statements, invoices and bills
  • Bank, GST and vendor reconciliations
  • Simple monthly reports: sales, expenses, profit, receivables and payables
  • Payroll processing and salary statements

Year-end and reporting

  • Financial statements in the required format for companies, LLPs, firms and proprietors
  • Supporting schedules and working papers for year-end finalisation
  • Books for a new company's first year, set up properly from day one

For loans and funding

  • Projected financial statements and project reports

  • Working-capital and repayment projections that a bank can follow

For overseas accounting practices

  • Outsourced bookkeeping and payroll in Xero for small practices in Australia and elsewhere, handled by our team in India

Questions we often get

Which accounting software do you work with?

Tally Prime, Zoho Books and Xero. If you don't use any software yet, we will suggest one that suits the size of your business and set it up.

Can you prepare a project report for my bank loan?

Yes. We prepare projected financial statements, project reports and CMA data based on your actual numbers and a realistic plan, in the format banks expect.

I only have bank statements. Can you still do my accounts?

Yes. Many of our clients start that way. We build the books from the bank statements and ask you about the entries we can't identify.
