Questions we often get

When is the due date for filing my income tax return?

For most individuals and small businesses, the due date is 31 July after the year ends. Companies and larger businesses usually have until 31 October. Dates are sometimes extended, so check our compliance calendar or ask us.

I received an income tax notice. What should I do?

Don't ignore it and don't reply in a hurry. Send us the notice. We will tell you what it means, what the deadline is, and prepare the reply with the right documents.

Do you file TDS returns for small businesses and associations?

Yes. We file TDS returns for companies, firms, doctors, societies and associations. Missed TDS returns attract a late fee of ₹200 a day, so it is worth getting them up to date.
