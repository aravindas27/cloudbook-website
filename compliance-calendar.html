Compliance calendar 2026-27: GST, TDS, income tax and ROC due dates

Every regular due date for businesses, companies and LLPs in India, from April 2026 to March 2027, on one page. Bookmark it, or let us track it for you.

These are the standard statutory due dates. The government sometimes extends them, and some dates depend on your AGM or quarterly filing choice. The new Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026. Please check with us before relying on a date.

Late fees in brief

  • GST returns: late fee per day of delay, plus 18% interest on tax paid late
  • TDS returns: ₹200 a day until the return is filed, up to the TDS amount
  • ROC forms (AOC-4, MGT-7, LLP Form 8 and 11): ₹100 a day per form, with no upper limit
  • DIR-3 KYC: ₹5,000 once the due date passes
  • Belated income tax return: a fee of up to ₹5,000, plus interest on tax due
